📖 Reading — Jenny

Reading focus: Follow Fritz's discovery of the message, Jenny's rescue, the arrival of the English ship and the family's final decisions.

📝 Summary

Fritz explores an unfamiliar cove and discovers oysters containing valuable pearls. Later, he finds a message tied to a seabird's leg asking people to save an English woman from the smoking rock.

At first, Father doubts the message because he thinks it may be old and the woman may already be dead. However, Fritz notices smoke rising from a distant island and decides to investigate.

After five days, Fritz returns with Jenny Montrose. She explains that she has survived alone on the island for three years. She built a hut, kept a fire burning to attract attention and trained a bird to bring her food and carry her message.

The family welcomes Jenny with a feast. Later, they hear gunshots and discover an English ship. Its captain knows Jenny's father, Colonel Montrose, and has been searching for her.

Father and Mother decide to remain in New Switzerland. Ernest and Jack stay with them, while Fritz, Jenny and Franz prepare to return to Europe. Fritz hopes to marry Jenny, and Father gives him the journal of their adventures.

👨‍👩‍👦 Characters

Father

The narrator. He is cautious about the message, welcomes Jenny and helps the family decide their future.

Fritz

The eldest son. He discovers the message, searches for Jenny, rescues her and plans to return to Europe with her.

Jenny Montrose

A British young woman who survives alone on an island for three years before Fritz rescues her.

Mother

A caring and practical woman who welcomes Jenny and chooses to remain in New Switzerland.

Ernest

One of the sons. He decides to stay with his parents in New Switzerland.

Jack

One of the sons. He also chooses to remain on the island with his family.

Franz

The youngest son. He wants to attend a good school and leaves with Fritz and Jenny.

The English Captain

A friendly captain who knows Jenny's father and has been searching for her.

Colonel Montrose

Jenny's father, a British officer who never gives up hope of finding his daughter.

Jenny's Bird

A trained seabird that brings Jenny food and carries the message that eventually leads to her rescue.

💡 Main Themes

Hope and Survival

Jenny keeps trying to attract attention and never gives up hope of being rescued.

Exploration and Discovery

Fritz's curiosity leads him to discover pearls, the message and the island where Jenny lives.

Family and Belonging

The family welcomes Jenny and considers each son's wishes when deciding who will stay and who will leave.

Communication

The message on the bird's leg connects Jenny with Fritz and leads to her rescue.

Love and New Beginnings

Fritz falls in love with Jenny, and their departure begins a new stage in their lives.

Home and Identity

Father and Mother choose New Switzerland as their permanent home and hope it will become a prosperous colony.
