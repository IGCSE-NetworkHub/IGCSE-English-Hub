📖 Reading — Exploring the Island

📝 Summary

The family realises that although they have lived on the island for three years, they have not explored all of it. They begin an expedition beyond the familiar rocks, cross a desert and discover a group of ostriches.

The family tries to catch an ostrich, but the dogs frighten the birds away and Ernest's eagle kills the male. They discover more than twenty ostrich eggs and mark the place so they can return later.

The explorers reach a beautiful valley called Green Glen, where Ernest encounters two bears. The family fights the dangerous animals and finally kills both. They return later to skin the bears and preserve the meat.

The older boys capture two rabbits and two fawns and drive a herd of antelope closer to Rock House. Jack also captures a live ostrich with a lasso and calms it with a coat. He hopes to tame it like a saddle horse.

👨‍👩‍👦 Characters

The Narrator (Father)

Leads the expedition and teaches the boys about nature and survival.

Fritz

The eldest son; helps explore and fight the bears.

Ernest

Releases his eagle to hunt and later has a frightening encounter with bears.

Jack

Finds the ostrich eggs and captures a live ostrich with a lasso.

Franz

Stays at camp with his mother and helps with the bear skins.

Mother

Prepares meals and helps clean, salt and dry the bear skins.

Juno and the Other Dogs

Juno stays at camp; the other dogs accompany the explorers and attack the bears.

Knips and Ernest's Eagle

Knips joins the expedition; the eagle kills the male ostrich.

The Animals

The ostriches, bears, rabbits, fawns and antelope play important roles in the expedition.

💡 Main Themes

Exploration and Curiosity

The family wants to discover parts of the island they have never seen.

Adventure and Survival

The journey includes a desert, wild animals and dangerous encounters.

Resourcefulness

The family uses practical knowledge and available resources to survive.

Family Cooperation

Family members contribute to exploring, hunting, cooking and preparing resources.

Humans and Nature

The family must adapt to and defend themselves against the natural world.
