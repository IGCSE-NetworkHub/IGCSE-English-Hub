Welcome to Skills Development

This centre is designed for students who want to improve specific English skills. Choose the skill you want to develop and follow a structured learning programme at your own level.

You can join one programme or develop several skills independently of your school year.

Choose Your Skill

Reading Development

Improve reading comprehension, vocabulary in context, inference, analysis and answering techniques.

Writing Development

Develop sentence structure, paragraph organisation, grammar accuracy and different writing styles.

Speaking Development

Improve pronunciation, fluency, communication, vocabulary and confidence when speaking English.

Listening Development

Improve listening comprehension, identifying key information, understanding details and note-taking.

Live Sessions

Take part in interactive live sessions designed to help you practise English with your teacher and other students.

Live session access links will be displayed after registration and administrative approval. Sessions will be delivered through an external meeting platform.
