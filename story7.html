📖 Reading — Giving Thanks for Our Blessings

The next day was an important day for us. It marked our second anniversary on the island. Father decided that we should celebrate by making it a holiday.

We had a great feast and held contests in wrestling, running, swimming, shooting and horseback riding. The boys had been practising these activities for two years.

Fritz was the champion swimmer and the best shot. His prizes were an English rifle and a hunting knife. Ernest was the best runner, so he received a gold watch.

Jack was the best at riding the donkey. He won a pair of silver spurs and a riding whip. Franz won the buffalo-riding contest and received stirrups and a driving whip.

Father also gave Mother a lovely workbox containing fine needles and sewing supplies. She was very pleased with her gift.

The family celebrated their second anniversary by remembering their blessings, enjoying each other's company and rewarding the boys for their hard work.

The next day we returned to our usual work because the rainy season was approaching. The boys caught fish and preserved them by salting them. The salt from the cave made this easy.

We also gathered bags of sweet acorns to store for the winter.

Jack decided to make a private expedition to the swamp to collect reeds for baskets and hen coops. He went farther into the swamp because the reeds there were better.

Suddenly, Jack's foot slipped and he sank into the thick, soft mud. At first he was only over his ankles, but soon he had sunk as far as his knees.

Jack called for help, but nobody heard him. Then Fangs, his faithful jackal, appeared. Fangs could not pull Jack out by himself, but he ran backwards and forwards between Jack and the bank.

Jack cut the reeds that he could reach and tied them into a bundle. He leaned on the bundle and managed to free his legs. Then he got astride the reeds.

Fangs ran towards the bank. Jack grabbed Fangs by the tail and allowed the reeds to act like a raft. Fangs pulled him towards dry land.

"Fangs is a hero — it's a good thing he was with you!"

When Jack returned, he was muddy and exhausted. Father was relieved that he was safe and praised Fangs for helping him.

Father was pleased with the reeds because he had a new plan. He wanted to build a loom for Mother.

A week later, Father gave Mother the loom. She was delighted because she could now weave cloth for clothing for the whole family.

The family continued preparing for winter. They gathered roots, fruits, grains, potatoes, rice, sweet acorns and pine cones and stored them in the cavern.

After all their hard work, the family was ready for another winter.

Through teamwork, cleverness, faith and gratitude, the family continued to build a safe life on the island.

📝 Summary

1. A Special Anniversary

The family celebrated their second anniversary on the island with a feast and several contests. Fritz won swimming and shooting, Ernest won running, Jack won donkey riding and Franz won buffalo riding. Father also gave Mother a beautiful workbox containing sewing materials.

2. Jack's Dangerous Expedition

As the rainy season approached, the family prepared food for winter. Jack went into the swamp to collect reeds but became trapped in deep, soft mud. Fangs, his faithful jackal, helped him escape. Jack used a bundle of reeds as a raft and held Fangs' tail while the jackal pulled him to safety.

3. A New Loom

Father used the reeds to make a loom for Mother. She was delighted because she could now weave cloth and make clothing for the family. The family continued gathering food and storing it in the cavern until they were ready for another winter.

💡 Main Theme

Gratitude and Ingenuity in the Face of Isolation

The family continues to survive by being thankful for what they have and by using their skills and imagination to solve problems.

  • Gratitude: They celebrate their blessings and survival.
  • Unity: The family works together.
  • Resourcefulness: They make useful things from available materials.
  • Resilience: They overcome difficult situations.
  • Inventiveness: Father turns reeds into a useful loom.
  • Cooperation: Jack and Fangs work together to escape.

Contests

Competitive activities with rules and a goal of winning.

Expert

Highly skilled or knowledgeable.

Stirrups

Metal loops on a saddle supporting a rider's feet.

Workbox

A container holding sewing materials and tools.

Preserved

Stored to prevent decay or spoilage.

Swamp

Wet, soft, muddy land often covered with water.

Slime

A slippery, sticky and unpleasant substance.

Ankles

The joints connecting the foot with the leg.

Marsh

Low wet land with reeds or grasses.

Struggled

Fought to escape or achieve something difficult.

Faithful

Loyal and reliable.

Raft

A flat floating structure used for support in water or soft ground.

Loom

A machine or frame for weaving thread or yarn into fabric.

Weave

To make fabric by crossing threads together.

Cavern

A large cave, typically underground.
